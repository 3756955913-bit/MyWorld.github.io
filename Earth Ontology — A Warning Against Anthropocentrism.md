* **Hypothesis A | Earth-Core Moderates:** the moderate consciousness within the Subterranean Indigenous that no longer wishes to Harvest (located in the Earth’s core / the consciousness core of the Opposition).

The two paths yield completely different endpoints. Once the true identity collapses into a single value, “closing the 25th station” becomes either inevitable or impossible — and ceases to be “a variable with suspense, with cost, requiring a variable to pry it open.”
